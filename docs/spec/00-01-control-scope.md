## 1. Scope and assumptions

### 1.1 Business context

The ticketing platform is "the primary access-control mechanism for Bashundhara Sports City and shall govern all visitors, members, and staff entry" (SRS §4 preamble).

**Success means a transactionally consistent platform covering booking, identity, payment, credential, physical access and finance reconciliation:**
- every successful purchase produces an auditable **entitlement**;
- the entitlement is verifiable at a gate in real time, or in controlled edge failover; and
- all revenue reconciles to the Finance ERP General Ledger with no duplicate or missing transactions (FR-TCK-010, SRS §30.2, §31.2).

**End-to-end journey:**
1. Register.
2. Establish identity and consent.
3. Discover a product or slot.
4. Read authoritative availability.
5. Hold inventory.
6. Price and tax.
7. Pay, or approve a zero-value pass.
8. Atomically confirm.
9. Issue one credential per attendee.
10. Notify (SMS / email / push).
11. Issue the tax invoice.
12. Verify at the gate (validity, zone, blacklist, re-entry policy).
13. Open or deny.
14. Log access.
15. Reconcile payment and revenue to GL.
